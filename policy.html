Privacy Policy

KotamuWisataku is a static site with no server and no database. There are no user accounts, and we run no analytics or advertising trackers.

Data stored in your browser

Two things are kept in your own browser storage, not on our servers:

  • kw-theme — your light or dark mode preference.
  • kw-subscribers — the email address you enter in the subscribe form.

Both disappear the moment you clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Location

The “Nearby Attractions” feature asks for location permission through your browser. Your coordinates are used on your device only, to measure distance to destinations. They are never sent to us and never stored. If you decline, the site still works using Jakarta as the reference point.

Third-party services

Loading this page means your browser also contacts the services below, so your IP address is visible to them:

  • OpenStreetMap and CARTO — map tiles.
  • Wikimedia Commons and LoremFlickr — destination photos.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and unpkg — fonts, icons, and the map library.
  • FormSubmit — only when you press subscribe; the email you enter is forwarded through this service to the site owner.
  • Google Maps — only when you press a directions button.

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Cookie Policy

This site sets no cookies. We also use no third-party cookies for advertising or measurement.

What we use is localStorage, browser-native storage that — unlike cookies — is not attached to every network request. It holds only the two items listed in the Privacy Policy above.

You can clear it at any time via “Clear site data” in your browser settings, without affecting anything else on the site.
